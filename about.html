Supporting the cystic fibrosis research community.

Our mission

The Cystic Fibrosis Microbiome Analysis Core facilitates best practices in microbiome research. The core develops and shares SOPs spanning sample collection and processing, bioinformatics, and biostatistics, and provides expert consulting and training across microbiome-based CF projects.
